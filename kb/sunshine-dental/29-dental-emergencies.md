# Dental Emergencies

If you have a dental emergency, call Sunshine Dental at (555) 000-9999 any time; the line is answered 24/7. This page explains how we handle emergencies during office hours and after hours, and when to go to the ER instead.

## When to go to the ER or call 911

Some situations need emergency medical care right away, not a dental appointment. Go to the ER or call 911 for:

- Swelling that affects breathing or swallowing
- Swelling spreading to the eye or neck
- Uncontrolled bleeding
- A jaw injury or major facial trauma
- High fever with facial swelling

## Dental emergencies during office hours

Our office is open Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. If you have a dental emergency during office hours, call us at (555) 000-9999.

Same-day emergency slots are held every weekday at 11:00 AM and 3:30 PM. An emergency visit usually starts with a limited exam:

- Limited / emergency exam (D0140): $75–$95

This is a self-pay range before insurance. If you're on the Sunshine Smile Plan, 1 emergency exam per year is included.

## Dental emergencies after hours and on weekends

Our phone line is answered 24/7 by the practice's voice assistant. After hours and on weekends:

- The voice assistant takes the details of your emergency.
- Dr. Mei Chen is on call for patients of record.
- Dr. Chen returns urgent calls within 1 hour between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM.

We are closed on Saturday and Sunday and on these holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the Friday after, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day.

## Knocked-out adult tooth

A knocked-out adult tooth is time-sensitive. Within 60 minutes matters most.

- Handle the tooth by the crown (the part you normally see), not the root.
- Keep the tooth in milk, or gently place it back in the socket.
- Come in or call us right away at (555) 000-9999.

## Emergencies for non-patients

You don't have to be a current patient to get emergency dental care at Sunshine Dental. We see emergencies for non-patients in our same-day slots (11:00 AM and 3:30 PM on weekdays) when they're available. Please call first so we can check availability. Dr. Chen's after-hours on-call callbacks are for patients of record.

Stay calm, call us, and we'll help you figure out the fastest next step.
