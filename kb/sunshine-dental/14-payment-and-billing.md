# Payment and Billing

Here's how payment works at Sunshine Dental: when payment is due, which payment methods we accept, and how statements and refunds are handled.

## When payment is due

At Sunshine Dental, **your estimated share is due at the time of service**. If you have insurance, we verify your benefits 2 business days before your visit and give you an estimate of your share. If you don't have insurance, you pay our self-pay fee for the care you receive.

For example, our self-pay prices before insurance include:

- Periodic exam (D0120): $55–$75
- Adult cleaning (D1110): $105–$135
- Four bitewing x-rays (D0274): $65–$85

## Payment methods we accept

Sunshine Dental accepts:

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover
- HSA/FSA cards
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Cash
- Personal checks

## Statements and balances

After your insurance processes a claim, you may owe a remaining balance. Sunshine Dental sends **statements by email**, and the **balance is due within 30 days**.

Remember that an insurance estimate isn't a guarantee: your plan decides what it pays, so the amount you owe after the claim can differ from the estimate.

## Credit balance refunds

Sometimes you may pay more than you end up owing, for example if your insurance pays more than estimated. When you have a credit balance with Sunshine Dental, we **refund it within 30 days on request**. Just ask our office.

## Help paying for larger treatment

If you're facing a larger treatment cost, Sunshine Dental has options:

- **CareCredit** (6–12 month promotional plans for qualifying patients) and **Sunbit**. You can apply at the front desk or online, with a decision in minutes.
- **In-house payment plan** for treatment over $1,000: one third down, the rest in 3 interest-free monthly payments, with a card on file.

If you don't have dental insurance, our Sunshine Smile Plan ($349/year adults, $279/year children under 14) includes 2 exams, 2 regular cleanings, all needed x-rays, 1 emergency exam per year, and 20% off other treatment.
