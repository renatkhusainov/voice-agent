# Teeth Whitening and Cosmetic Dentistry

Want a brighter smile? Sunshine Dental offers in-office whitening, take-home whitening trays, and a free cosmetic consultation to help you decide what fits your goals.

## In-office teeth whitening

In-office whitening: $450–$550, done in one 90-minute visit.

In-office whitening is the fastest way to brighten your teeth. Dr. Mei Chen applies a professional whitening treatment to your teeth while you relax in the chair, and the whole treatment is finished the same day. Because it's all done in one 90-minute visit, it's a good choice if you have an event coming up or simply prefer not to whiten at home.

Prices on this page are self-pay ranges before insurance.

## Take-home whitening trays

Take-home whitening trays: $250–$325.

Take-home whitening uses custom trays made to fit your teeth, which you fill with whitening gel and wear at home. Trays let you whiten gradually on your own schedule. Our hygienist, Sam Rivera, RDH, helps with whitening trays.

## In-office whitening vs. take-home trays

- In-office whitening is quicker: one 90-minute visit, $450–$550.
- Take-home trays cost less ($250–$325) and work over time at home.

Not sure which is right for you? That's exactly what the free cosmetic consultation is for.

## Free 20-minute cosmetic consultation

Sunshine Dental offers a free 20-minute cosmetic consultation. During the cosmetic consultation, you can talk about what you'd like to change about your smile, and Dr. Chen will explain which options make sense for you. There's no cost and no obligation.

## Other cosmetic options

Whitening isn't the only way to improve how your smile looks. Depending on your goals, Dr. Chen may talk with you about:

- Clear aligners (D8090, $3,800–$5,500) for mild to moderate crowding or spacing, with a separate free aligner consultation
- Tooth-colored fillings, which blend in with your natural teeth
- Porcelain/ceramic crowns (D2740, $1,150–$1,450) for damaged or misshapen teeth
