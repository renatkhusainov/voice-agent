# Dental Crowns

A crown covers and protects a tooth that is too damaged or weakened for a filling. This page explains what a crown is, how the two crown visits work, and what crowns cost at Sunshine Dental.

## What a dental crown is

A dental crown is a cap that fits over the whole visible part of a tooth. A crown restores the tooth's shape and strength and protects what's left of it. Dr. Mei Chen may recommend a crown for a tooth with a large cavity, a crack, a large old filling, or after a root canal on a back tooth.

## Crown prices

- Porcelain/ceramic crown (D2740): $1,150–$1,450
- Core buildup (D2950): $250–$325

These are self-pay ranges before insurance. We verify your benefits 2 business days before your visit and give you an estimate, though your plan decides what it pays.

## What a core buildup is

A core buildup (D2950) rebuilds the inside of a tooth when too much of it is missing to support a crown on its own. Think of it as a solid foundation the crown can sit on. Not every crown needs a core buildup; Dr. Chen decides based on how much healthy tooth remains, and she'll tell you before treatment if one is part of your plan.

## Two visits about 2 weeks apart

A porcelain/ceramic crown (D2740) at Sunshine Dental takes two visits, about 2 weeks apart.

- First crown visit: Dr. Chen prepares the tooth, records its shape so the permanent crown can be made, and places a temporary crown.
- Between visits: you wear the temporary crown, which protects the tooth while your permanent crown is being made.
- Second crown visit: Dr. Chen removes the temporary crown, checks the fit and color of the permanent crown, and places it.

When you book your first crown visit, it's a good idea to schedule the second one at the same time.

## Crowns after a root canal on a back tooth

A crown is usually needed after a root canal on a back tooth, such as a premolar or molar. The crown is a separate fee from the root canal. Front-tooth (D3310) and premolar (D3320) root canals are done by Dr. Chen at our office, and molar root canals (D3330) are referred to Tampa Endodontics; either way, Dr. Chen can place the crown here afterward.

Crowns on dental implants are a different service. Dr. Chen restores implants with an abutment and implant crown (D6057 + D6065), priced at $2,100–$2,600 together.
