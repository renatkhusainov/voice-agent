# Root Canals

A root canal can save a tooth whose inner tissue is infected or badly inflamed. At Sunshine Dental, Dr. Chen treats front teeth and premolars, and molar root canals are referred to a trusted specialist.

## What a root canal is

Inside every tooth is soft tissue called the pulp, which contains nerves and blood vessels. When the pulp becomes infected or badly inflamed, a root canal removes it, cleans and disinfects the inside of the tooth, and fills and seals the space. The goal of a root canal is to keep your natural tooth rather than remove it.

Only a dentist can decide whether a tooth needs a root canal. Dr. Mei Chen will examine the tooth, review x-rays, and explain her recommendation.

## Molar root canals: referred to Tampa Endodontics

Root canals on molars (D3330), the large back teeth, are referred to our partner, Tampa Endodontics. Tampa Endodontics sets its own fee for a molar root canal, typically $1,200–$1,500.

After a molar root canal at Tampa Endodontics, you come back to Sunshine Dental for the rest of your care, including a crown if one is needed.

## A crown after a back-tooth root canal

A crown is usually needed after a root canal on a back tooth (a premolar or molar). The crown is a separate fee from the root canal.

- Porcelain/ceramic crown (D2740): $1,150–$1,450, placed over two visits about 2 weeks apart, with a temporary crown in between
- Core buildup (D2950), if needed: $250–$325

Dr. Chen will let you know whether your tooth needs a crown and a core buildup.
