# Tooth Extractions and Wisdom Teeth

Sometimes a tooth can't be saved and needs to come out. Dr. Chen handles simple and surgical extractions at Sunshine Dental, and impacted wisdom teeth are referred to an oral surgery partner.

## Simple extraction (D7140)

Simple extraction (D7140): $175–$250.

A simple extraction removes a tooth that is fully visible above the gums and can be lifted out without surgery. Dr. Mei Chen performs simple extractions at our office.

Prices on this page are self-pay ranges before insurance, and we'll give you an estimate before treatment.

## Surgical extraction (D7210)

Surgical extraction (D7210): $295–$395.

A surgical extraction is needed when a tooth is broken at the gumline, has curved or stubborn roots, or can't be removed in one piece. The tooth may need to be sectioned or the gum gently opened to reach it. Dr. Chen performs surgical extractions at Sunshine Dental too. She decides which type of extraction your tooth needs after examining it and reviewing x-rays.

## Impacted wisdom teeth (D7240): Bayshore Oral Surgery

Wisdom teeth are the last molars at the very back of the mouth. When a wisdom tooth is impacted, meaning it's stuck in the jawbone or under the gums and can't come in normally, removal is a job for a specialist.

Impacted wisdom teeth (D7240) are referred to our partner, Bayshore Oral Surgery. Dr. Chen may take a panoramic x-ray (D0330, $120–$150), which shows all your teeth and jaws in one image, to see how your wisdom teeth are positioned.

## IV sedation for wisdom teeth

IV sedation is not offered at Sunshine Dental. If you want or need IV sedation for wisdom tooth removal, it is available at Bayshore Oral Surgery.

For extractions done at our office, you can choose from:

- Our comfort menu: headphones, blankets, and breaks whenever you raise a hand
- Nitrous oxide (D9230): $75–$95 per visit; you can drive yourself home afterward
- Oral conscious sedation (D9248): $250–$350 per visit; requires an adult driver and a pre-sedation review with Dr. Chen at a prior visit

## Before and after an extraction

Before your extraction, tell us about any blood thinners, heart conditions, joint replacements, diabetes, or recent medication changes. Some heart conditions and joint replacements need antibiotics before treatment; your physician or Dr. Chen decides.

Dr. Chen will explain what to expect after your extraction before you leave the office.

## Replacing a missing tooth

After an extraction, you may want to replace the tooth. Dental implants are placed at Bayshore Oral Surgery and restored by Dr. Chen with an abutment and implant crown (D6057 + D6065), $2,100–$2,600 together. Ask Dr. Chen about your options at your visit.
