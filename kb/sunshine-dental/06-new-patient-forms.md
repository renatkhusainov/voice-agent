# New Patient Forms

Before your first visit, Sunshine Dental asks you to complete a few forms. Filling them in online ahead of time saves you time at the office.

## The four new patient forms

Every new patient at Sunshine Dental completes four forms:

1. **Medical history:** your health conditions, medications, and allergies, so your dental care can be planned safely.
2. **HIPAA acknowledgment:** confirms you've received our notice of privacy practices, which explains how your health information is protected.
3. **Financial policy:** explains how payment works, such as your estimated share being due at the time of service.
4. **Treatment consent:** your agreement to receive dental care at our office.

## How you receive your new patient forms

Your new patient forms are sent to you by **text and email 48 hours before your visit**. The message includes a link to our patient portal at portal.sunshinedental.example, where you can fill in the forms online from your phone or computer.

If you don't see the message, check your spam or junk folder, or call us at (555) 000-9999 and we can help.

## If you haven't completed your forms online

If you don't finish your new patient forms online before your visit, please **arrive 30 minutes early** so you have time to fill them in at the office. If your forms are already done, arriving 15 minutes early is enough.

Arriving early matters because a new patient visit takes 60–90 minutes, and starting on time keeps your full appointment available for your exam, x-rays, and cleaning.

## Your HIPAA notice of privacy practices

You'll receive our HIPAA notice of privacy practices at your first visit, and a copy is available any time on request. Sunshine Dental never shares your records without your written authorization, except as the law allows (for example, for treatment or payment).

## Updating your medical history at every visit

Your medical history isn't a one-time form. Please update it at every visit, and tell us about:

- Pregnancy
- Heart conditions
- Joint replacements
- Blood thinners
- Diabetes
- Any changes in your medications
- Allergies, such as to latex or local anesthetic

Some heart conditions and joint replacements need antibiotics before dental treatment. Whether you need them is decided by your physician or Dr. Chen, so please tell us before your visit. Our office is latex-free, and allergies are noted in your chart.

## Need help with the forms?

If you have trouble opening the portal link or have a question about a form, call (555) 000-9999 or email frontdesk@sunshinedental.example and our team will help you get it sorted.
