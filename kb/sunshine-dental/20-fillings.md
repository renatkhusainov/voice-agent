# Tooth-Colored Fillings

When a tooth has a cavity, a filling repairs it and restores its shape. At Sunshine Dental, every filling we place is tooth-colored.

## What a dental filling is

A dental filling repairs a tooth that has been damaged by decay (a cavity) or a small break. Dr. Mei Chen removes the damaged part of the tooth and fills the space with a material that seals it and restores its shape, so you can chew normally again.

## Tooth-colored filling prices

Our fillings are priced by location and by how many surfaces of the tooth are involved:

- Tooth-colored filling, 1 surface, back tooth (D2391): $175–$225
- Tooth-colored filling, 2 surfaces, back tooth (D2392): $220–$280
- Tooth-colored filling, 1 surface, front tooth (D2330): $160–$210

These are self-pay ranges before insurance. A "surface" is one side of the tooth, such as the chewing surface or the side facing the next tooth. Dr. Chen will tell you which type of filling you need, and we'll give you an estimate before treatment.

## No silver (amalgam) fillings

Sunshine Dental does not place silver (amalgam) fillings. All new fillings are tooth-colored, made from a material matched to the shade of your natural teeth so they blend in. If you have older silver fillings from another office, Dr. Chen can look at them during your exam and talk with you about them.

## What to expect at a filling visit

Every filling is a little different, but a typical filling visit with Dr. Chen goes like this:

- Dr. Chen numbs the area around the tooth so you're comfortable.
- She removes the decay and cleans the space.
- The tooth-colored material is placed, usually in layers, and hardened with a special light.
- She shapes and polishes the filling and checks your bite.

If you've ever had a reaction to latex or local anesthetic, please tell us; allergies are noted in your chart, and our office is latex-free. Dr. Chen will let you know anything you need to know after your visit.

## Feeling nervous about a filling

If a filling visit makes you anxious, you have options. Our comfort menu includes headphones, blankets, and breaks whenever you raise a hand. Nitrous oxide (D9230, $75–$95 per visit) is also available, and you can drive yourself home afterward.

## When a filling isn't enough

If a cavity or crack is too large for a filling, a filling may not be strong enough to hold the tooth together. In that case Dr. Chen may recommend a crown (D2740) instead. She'll explain why and walk you through your options.
