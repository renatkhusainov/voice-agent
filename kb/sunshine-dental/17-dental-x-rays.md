# Dental X-Rays at Sunshine Dental

Dental x-rays let Dr. Chen see what can't be seen just by looking in your mouth, such as decay between teeth and the bone around your roots. Here are the types of x-rays we take, what they cost, and our policies for pregnancy and records from other offices.

## Why dental x-rays are taken

Dental x-rays show areas hidden from view: the spaces between your back teeth, the roots of your teeth, and the jawbone around them. They help Dr. Mei Chen find problems like cavities early and plan treatment. Dr. Chen recommends x-rays based on what she needs to see for your care.

The prices below are self-pay ranges before insurance.

## Bitewing x-rays (D0274)

Four bitewing x-rays (D0274): $65–$85.

Bitewing x-rays are small images of your back teeth, taken while you gently bite on a holder. Bitewings are especially good at showing decay between the back teeth, where a toothbrush and the naked eye can't reach. They are a common part of routine checkups.

## Full-mouth x-rays (D0210)

Full-mouth x-rays (D0210): $140–$180.

A full-mouth series is a set of x-rays that together show every tooth, from crown to root tip. Full x-rays are part of our new patient visit, along with a comprehensive exam and a cleaning if your gums are healthy. They give Dr. Chen a complete starting picture of your mouth.

## Panoramic x-ray (D0330)

Panoramic x-ray (D0330): $120–$150.

A panoramic x-ray is a single wide image of your upper and lower jaws, taken by a machine that moves around your head. A panoramic x-ray shows all your teeth, your jaw joints, and teeth still under the gums, such as wisdom teeth, in one view.

## Dental x-rays during pregnancy

If you are pregnant or might be, please tell us. Our policy during pregnancy is to take dental x-rays only when they are needed, and with shielding. Routine cleanings are fine during pregnancy. Please update us at every visit if anything about your pregnancy or health changes.

## Bringing x-rays from your previous dentist

If you had dental x-rays at another office less than 12 months ago, ask that office to send them to us before your visit. If they are recent and clear enough, we may not need to take new ones.

Bringing a treatment plan and x-rays less than 6 months old from another office also qualifies you for a free second-opinion visit, with the limited exam fee waived.

If you ever need copies of your own x-rays sent to another dentist, request them at the front desk or through the patient portal. Copies sent to another dentist are free and ready within 5 business days.

## X-rays and your insurance or Smile Plan

Coverage for dental x-rays depends on your plan; we verify your benefits 2 business days before your visit and give you an estimate. If you are on the Sunshine Smile Plan, all needed x-rays are included in your yearly membership.
