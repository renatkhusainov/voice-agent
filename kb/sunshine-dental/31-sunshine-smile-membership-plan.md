# Sunshine Smile Plan: our membership plan for patients without insurance

No dental insurance? The Sunshine Smile Plan is our in-house membership. You pay one yearly fee, your routine care is covered, and other treatment is discounted.

## What the Sunshine Smile Plan costs

- Adults: $349 per year
- Children under 14: $279 per year

The plan starts the day you sign up. You can join at the front desk or by asking us when you book.

## What the Sunshine Smile Plan includes

Each year of membership includes:

- 2 exams
- 2 regular cleanings
- All x-rays you need
- 1 emergency exam
- 20% off all other treatment, such as fillings, crowns, root canals, and whitening

For comparison, without insurance or membership an adult cleaning (D1110) is $105–$135 and a periodic exam (D0120) is $55–$75 each time, before x-rays. Those are self-pay ranges.

## No waiting periods, no annual maximum

Unlike insurance, the Sunshine Smile Plan has:

- No waiting periods: use it the day you join
- No annual maximum: the 20% discount applies however much treatment you need
- No pre-existing condition exclusions

## How the membership plan works with insurance

The Sunshine Smile Plan isn't insurance, and it can't be combined with an insurance plan. If you have dental insurance we accept, your plan's benefits usually make more sense. If your plan isn't one we take (for example a DHMO or a Florida Medicaid dental plan), the membership plan is an option many of our patients choose instead.

## Paying for bigger treatment as a member

Members can still use our other payment options for the remaining 80% of larger treatment: CareCredit, Sunbit, or our in-house plan for treatment over $1,000 (one third down, then 3 interest-free monthly payments).
