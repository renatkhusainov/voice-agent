# Sedation and Dental Anxiety

Feeling nervous about the dentist is very common, and you don't have to just push through it. Sunshine Dental offers a comfort menu, nitrous oxide, and oral conscious sedation, and we can refer you for IV sedation when needed.

## Our comfort menu

Everyone at Sunshine Dental can use our comfort menu, at no extra charge:

- Headphones, so you can listen to music or tune out sounds
- Blankets to keep you cozy in the chair
- Breaks on request: just raise a hand whenever you need a pause

Let us know when you book that you feel anxious, and we'll make sure your care team knows too.

## Nitrous oxide ("laughing gas")

Nitrous oxide (D9230): $75–$95 per visit.

Prices on this page are self-pay ranges before insurance.

Nitrous oxide, often called laughing gas, is a mild sedative you breathe in through a small mask over your nose. It helps you feel more relaxed during treatment while you stay awake. With nitrous oxide, you can drive yourself home after your appointment.

## Oral conscious sedation

Oral conscious sedation (D9248): $250–$350 per visit.

Oral conscious sedation is medication taken by mouth to help you feel deeply relaxed during treatment. Oral conscious sedation takes more planning than nitrous oxide:

- You need an adult driver to bring you to the appointment and take you home afterward.
- You need a pre-sedation review with Dr. Mei Chen at a prior visit, before the day of treatment.
- You'll get written instructions to follow.

Please follow those written instructions exactly, and call us with any questions about them.

## IV sedation at Bayshore Oral Surgery

IV sedation is not offered at Sunshine Dental. If you need or prefer IV sedation, it is available through our referral partner, Bayshore Oral Surgery, which also handles impacted wisdom teeth and implant placement.

## Choosing a sedation option

Which option is right depends on your treatment, your health, and how anxious you feel. Dr. Chen decides with you which sedation is appropriate. To help her plan safely, tell us about:

- Pregnancy
- Heart conditions or joint replacements
- Blood thinners
- Diabetes
- Any medication changes
- Allergies, including latex or local anesthetic (our office is latex-free)

Please update this information at every visit.

## Other ways we help nervous patients

Small things can make a big difference. Dr. Chen speaks English and Mandarin, Lucia Gómez speaks Spanish on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and a phone interpreter service is available any day at no cost, so you can ask questions in the language you're most comfortable with. Service animals are welcome.
