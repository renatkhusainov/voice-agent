# Dental Implants

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth with an artificial root and a crown that looks and works like a natural tooth. At Sunshine Dental, implants are a team effort between our oral surgery partner and Dr. Chen.

## What a dental implant is

A dental implant has three parts:

- The implant post, a small post placed in the jawbone that acts like a tooth root
- The abutment, a connector that attaches to the implant post
- The implant crown, the visible tooth-shaped part that sits on top

Together, these parts replace a single missing tooth without relying on the teeth next to it.

## Implant placement at Bayshore Oral Surgery

The implant post is placed by our referral partner, Bayshore Oral Surgery. Sunshine Dental does not place implants in-house. Bayshore Oral Surgery handles the surgical part of your implant care, and IV sedation is also available there if you need it.

The fee for implant placement is not part of our price range below. Bayshore Oral Surgery can give you their own estimate for placement.

## Dr. Chen restores your implant

Once the implant post is placed, Dr. Mei Chen restores it here at Sunshine Dental, meaning she attaches the abutment and the implant crown.

- Abutment (D6057) + implant crown (D6065): $2,100–$2,600 together

This is a self-pay range before insurance. We verify your benefits 2 business days before your visit and give you an estimate, though your plan decides what it pays.

## Implant timeline

Dental implant treatment happens in stages:

1. Implant placement at Bayshore Oral Surgery.
2. A healing period while the implant settles into the jawbone.
3. Dr. Chen places the abutment and implant crown at Sunshine Dental, 3–6 months after placement.

Bayshore Oral Surgery and Dr. Chen will let you know when you're ready for each step.

## Is a dental implant right for you?

Whether an implant is a good option depends on your mouth and your health, and only Dr. Chen and the surgeon can decide that. Start with an exam at Sunshine Dental. Please tell us about heart conditions, joint replacements, blood thinners, diabetes, and any medication changes, since these can matter for planning.

If another office has already recommended an implant, you can get a free second-opinion visit here (limited exam fee waived) when you bring the treatment plan and x-rays less than 6 months old.

## Paying for implant restoration

Implant restoration is one of our larger treatments, and there are several ways to pay over time:

- In-house payment plan for treatment over $1,000: one third down, the rest in 3 interest-free monthly payments, with a card on file
- CareCredit, with 6–12 month promotional plans for qualifying patients
- Sunbit

You can apply for CareCredit or Sunbit at the front desk or online and get a decision in minutes. Treatment plans with estimates are valid for 90 days, which gives you time to plan.
