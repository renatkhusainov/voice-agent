# Night Guards and Teeth Grinding

Many people grind or clench their teeth without realizing it, often while they sleep. A custom night guard from Sunshine Dental helps protect your teeth from that wear.

## What teeth grinding is

Teeth grinding (sometimes called bruxism) means clenching your jaw or rubbing your teeth together. It often happens during sleep, so many people don't know they do it. Some people notice things like:

- A partner hearing grinding sounds at night
- A sore or tired jaw in the morning
- Teeth that look worn, flattened, or chipped

These signs don't always mean you grind your teeth. Only Dr. Mei Chen can tell you whether a night guard makes sense for you, so mention any concerns at your exam.

## What a night guard is

A night guard is a custom-fitted appliance you wear over your teeth, usually while you sleep. It sits between your upper and lower teeth so they press against the guard instead of against each other. The night guard we make at Sunshine Dental is hard and covers the full arch (all the teeth on one jaw).

## How a custom night guard is made

A custom night guard starts with Dr. Chen taking impressions or a digital scan of your teeth. The night guard is then made from those records to fit your mouth. Once it's ready, Dr. Chen checks the fit with you in the office and will explain how to care for it.

## Who a night guard is for

A night guard is generally for people who grind or clench their teeth and want to protect them from wear. Dr. Chen may suggest a night guard after noticing signs of grinding at a checkup, or you can ask about one if you've noticed symptoms yourself.

A night guard is also different from other trays we make:

- Whitening trays hold whitening gel and are used for a short time.
- Clear aligners (D8090) move teeth.
- A night guard (D9944) protects teeth and doesn't move them.
