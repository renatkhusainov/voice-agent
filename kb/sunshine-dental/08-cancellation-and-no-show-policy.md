# Cancellation and No-Show Policy

Life happens, and Sunshine Dental understands that plans change. Our cancellation policy helps us keep appointment times open for patients who need them, including people waiting for urgent care.

## How much notice to give

Sunshine Dental asks for **at least 24 hours' notice** to cancel or reschedule an appointment.

To cancel or reschedule, call (555) 000-9999. Our phone is answered 24/7 by the practice's voice assistant, so you can reach us any time, even after hours. You can also reply R to a reminder text to ask for a reschedule call.

## The Monday appointment rule

Because Sunshine Dental is closed on Saturday and Sunday, Monday appointments have a special deadline. To cancel or reschedule a **Monday appointment, please let us know by Friday at 5:00 PM**.

## Your first late cancellation or no-show is forgiven

We know anyone can have an off day. At Sunshine Dental, your **first late cancellation or no-show is forgiven**, with no fee.

A late cancellation means canceling with less than 24 hours' notice (or after Friday at 5:00 PM for a Monday appointment). A no-show means missing the appointment without letting us know.

## The $50 late cancellation and no-show fee

After your first forgiven one, Sunshine Dental charges a **$50 fee for each missed appointment or late cancellation** (less than 24 hours' notice).

## Deposit after two no-shows in 12 months

If you have **two no-shows within 12 months**, a **$50 deposit is required to book** your next appointment. The deposit isn't an extra charge: it's applied to the cost of your visit.

## Weather closures are an exception

During hurricanes and tropical storms, Sunshine Dental follows Hillsborough County school closures. If we close for weather, we call everyone affected to reschedule, and **there are no fees** for appointments canceled because of a weather closure.

## Running late instead of canceling?

If you're just running behind, call us. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may mean we need to reschedule your appointment so the next patient isn't delayed. A quick call to (555) 000-9999 helps us plan.
