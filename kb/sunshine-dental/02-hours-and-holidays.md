# Office Hours and Holiday Closures

Here's when Sunshine Dental is open, which holidays we close for, and how to reach us when the office is closed. Our phone is answered around the clock, so you can always leave your details.

## Weekly office hours

Sunshine Dental is open:

- **Monday through Friday:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- **Saturday and Sunday:** closed

We don't close for lunch. The office stays open straight through the day, so a midday appointment is always an option on weekdays.

## Last appointment of the day

The last appointment of the day at Sunshine Dental starts at 4:30 PM. If you need an after-work time, ask for a 4:30 PM slot when you book.

## Holiday closures

Sunshine Dental is closed on these holidays:

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving and the Friday after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day

Our phone is still answered by the voice assistant on holidays, so you can leave your details any time.

## Phone answered 24/7

Our phone number, (555) 000-9999, is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week by the practice's voice assistant. Our staff are available from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

When the office is closed, after hours, or on weekends, the voice assistant takes your details. If you're a patient of record with an urgent problem, Dr. Chen is on call and returns urgent calls within 1 hour between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM.

## Same-day emergency slots

Sunshine Dental holds same-day emergency slots every weekday at 11:00 AM and 3:30 PM. Call as early as you can to ask for one. We also see people who aren't yet our patients in these slots when they're available.

Go to the ER or call 911 for swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, swelling spreading to the eye or neck, uncontrolled bleeding, a jaw injury or major facial trauma, or a high fever with facial swelling.

## Weather closures

During hurricanes and tropical storms, Sunshine Dental follows Hillsborough County school closures. If the county schools close, we close too, and we call everyone whose appointment is affected to reschedule. There are no cancellation fees for weather closures.
