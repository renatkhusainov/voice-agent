# Medicaid, CHIP, and Medicare

Government health programs handle dental care in different ways. This page explains which Medicaid, CHIP, and Medicare plans Sunshine Dental can and can't accept, and what options you have if your plan isn't accepted.

## Florida Medicaid dental plans

Sunshine Dental does not accept Florida Medicaid dental plans. This includes:

- DentaQuest
- MCNA
- Liberty

Because Sunshine Dental doesn't accept these plans, we can't bill a Florida Medicaid dental plan for your care. Call (555) 000-9999 to ask about our self-pay prices and the Sunshine Smile Plan.

## CHIP and Florida Healthy Kids dental

Sunshine Dental does not accept CHIP / Florida Healthy Kids dental coverage. Sunshine Dental sees children from age 3; families can ask about our self-pay prices and the Sunshine Smile Plan, which is $279/year for children under 14. Children under 3 are referred to Little Teeth Pediatric Dentistry.

## Original Medicare and dental care

Original Medicare doesn't cover routine dental care, such as checkups and cleanings. If you have only original Medicare, routine dental visits at Sunshine Dental would be self-pay, or you may want to consider our membership plan.

## Medicare Advantage plans with dental coverage

Some Medicare Advantage plans include dental benefits. At Sunshine Dental:

- **Medicare Advantage plans with a dental PPO through Delta Dental, Aetna, or Cigna** are treated like those PPOs.
- **Other Medicare Advantage dental plans:** we check case by case.

Call (555) 000-9999 with your plan card handy, and our team can look into your Medicare Advantage dental coverage. Our office manager, Priya Nair, handles insurance questions.

## The Sunshine Smile Plan as an alternative

If you don't have dental coverage we can accept, the Sunshine Smile Plan is our in-house membership plan:

- **$349/year** for adults
- **$279/year** for children under 14

The Sunshine Smile Plan includes 2 exams, 2 regular cleanings, all needed x-rays, 1 emergency exam per year, and 20% off other treatment. There are no waiting periods, no annual maximum, and no pre-existing condition exclusions. The plan starts the day you sign up.

The Sunshine Smile Plan is not insurance, and it can't be combined with insurance.

## Self-pay prices and financing

If you choose to pay on your own, here are some of our self-pay price ranges before insurance:

- Periodic exam (D0120): $55–$75
- Adult cleaning (D1110): $105–$135
- Child cleaning (D1120): $75–$95

For larger treatment, Sunshine Dental offers financing through CareCredit and Sunbit, and an in-house payment plan for treatment over $1,000.
