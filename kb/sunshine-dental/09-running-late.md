# Running Late to Your Appointment

Traffic, parking, and busy days can make anyone late. This page explains Sunshine Dental's late arrival rule and what to do if you're running behind.

## The 15-minute late arrival rule

At Sunshine Dental, arriving **more than 15 minutes late may mean rescheduling** your appointment. This isn't meant as a penalty. Each appointment is planned for a set amount of time, and starting late can delay the next patient's care.

If you arrive within 15 minutes of your appointment time, we'll do our best to see you as planned.

## What to do if you're running late

If you know you're going to be late to Sunshine Dental:

1. **Call us as soon as you can** at (555) 000-9999.
2. Let us know about how late you expect to be.
3. We'll tell you whether we can still see you or whether it's better to find a new time.

Calling ahead gives our team the chance to adjust the schedule, and sometimes that's enough to keep your appointment.

## Rescheduling when you're running late

If you'll be more than 15 minutes late, rescheduling is often the easiest option. Our office is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and the last appointment of the day starts at 4:30 PM.

If you decide you can't make it at all, please let us know as early as possible. Sunshine Dental asks for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule, and Monday appointments should be changed by Friday at 5:00 PM. Your first late cancellation or no-show is forgiven; after that, there's a $50 fee per missed appointment or late cancellation.

## Tips for arriving on time

A few things can help you get to Sunshine Dental on time:

- **Allow time for parking.** Our free garage is behind the building, with the entrance on S Albany Ave. Take a ticket, and we validate up to 3 hours at the front desk.
- **Know where to go.** We're in Suite 210 on the 2nd floor; take the elevator from the lobby.
- **Taking the bus?** The HART stop at Kennedy Blvd and Albany Ave is about 50 feet from the entrance.
- **New patients:** arrive 15 minutes early, or 30 minutes early if you haven't completed your forms online.

## Your reminders can help

Sunshine Dental sends text and email reminders 2 days and 2 hours before your appointment. The 2-hour reminder is a handy prompt to head out. If your plans have changed, reply R to ask for a reschedule call.
