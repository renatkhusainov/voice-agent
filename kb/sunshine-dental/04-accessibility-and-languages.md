# Accessibility and Language Support

Sunshine Dental wants every patient to feel comfortable and understood. This page covers wheelchair access, service animals, our latex-free office, and the languages we speak.

## Wheelchair access

The whole Sunshine Dental office is wheelchair accessible. Our office is Suite 210 on the 2nd floor, and there's an elevator from the building lobby.

If you drive, there are two accessible parking spaces on level 1 of the garage behind the building, next to the elevator. The garage entrance is on S Albany Ave, and we validate parking for up to 3 hours.

## Treatment room for wheelchair transfers

One of our treatment rooms has room for a wheelchair transfer to the dental chair. If you'd like that room, mention it when you book your appointment by calling (555) 000-9999.

## Service animals

Service animals are welcome at Sunshine Dental.

## Spanish language support

We're happy to help Spanish-speaking patients:

- **Lucia Gómez**, our patient coordinator, speaks Spanish and is in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- On any day, we can connect you with a **phone interpreter service at no cost** to you.

If you'd like to talk with Lucia in person, a Tuesday or Thursday appointment is a good choice. On other days, the free phone interpreter service is available.

## Mandarin language support

Dr. Mei Chen, our dentist and owner, speaks English and Mandarin. If you're more comfortable discussing your care in Mandarin, Dr. Chen can talk with you directly about your exam and treatment.

## Our latex-free office

Sunshine Dental is a latex-free office. If you have allergies, such as to latex or to local anesthetic, tell us and we'll note them in your chart. We ask you to review your medical history at every visit, so let us know about any new allergies too.

## Making your visit comfortable

Beyond access and language, we offer a comfort menu for any visit: headphones, blankets, and breaks on request. If you need a break during treatment, just raise a hand. Tell us what would help, and we'll do our best to make your visit easier.
