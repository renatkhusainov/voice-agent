# Your First Visit as a New Patient

Welcome to Sunshine Dental! This page explains what happens at your new patient visit, how long it takes, what to bring, and what each part costs before insurance.

## What the new patient visit includes

Your first appointment at Sunshine Dental is called a "new patient visit." It includes:

- **A comprehensive exam** with Dr. Mei Chen, a full check of your teeth and mouth so she can understand your overall dental health.
- **Full x-rays**, which let the dentist see things that aren't visible during a regular look, such as areas between teeth and below the gumline.
- **A cleaning, if your gums are healthy.** Whether a regular cleaning is right for you at this visit is decided by our team once your gums have been checked.

## How long the new patient visit takes

Plan for your new patient visit to take 60–90 minutes. Please arrive 15 minutes early so you can check in without feeling rushed. If you haven't completed your new patient forms online, arrive 30 minutes early instead so you have time to fill them in at the office.

## What to bring to your first visit

Please bring:

- A photo ID
- Your insurance card (if you have dental insurance)
- A list of your medications

Your new patient forms (medical history, HIPAA acknowledgment, financial policy, and treatment consent) are sent to you by text and email 48 hours before your visit, with a link to our patient portal.

## X-rays from your previous dentist

If you've had x-rays at another dental office in the last 12 months, ask that office to send them to us before your visit. With recent x-rays on hand, we may not need to take new ones.

## Children and teens at the first visit

Patients under 18 must come with a parent or legal guardian to their first visit. Sunshine Dental sees children from age 3. Children under 3 are referred to Little Teeth Pediatric Dentistry. Parents may stay with their children in the treatment room.

## Cost of the new patient visit

These are our self-pay price ranges before insurance for the parts of a new patient visit:

- Comprehensive exam (D0150): $95–$125
- Full-mouth x-rays (D0210): $140–$180
- Adult cleaning (D1110): $105–$135
- Child cleaning (D1120): $75–$95

If you have insurance, we verify your benefits 2 business days before your visit and give you an estimate. The estimate isn't a guarantee, because your plan decides what it pays. Your estimated share is due at the time of service.
