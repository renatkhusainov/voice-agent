# Financing and Payment Plans

Dental care shouldn't have to wait because of cost. Sunshine Dental offers outside financing and an in-house payment plan to help you spread out larger treatment costs.

## Financing options at a glance

Sunshine Dental offers three ways to pay over time:

- **CareCredit:** 6–12 month promotional plans for qualifying patients.
- **Sunbit:** financing you can apply for at our office or online.
- **In-house payment plan:** for treatment over $1,000, with one third down and the rest in 3 interest-free monthly payments.

## CareCredit financing

CareCredit is a healthcare financing option that Sunshine Dental accepts. CareCredit offers **6–12 month promotional plans for qualifying patients**. You can apply at the front desk or online, and you'll get a decision in minutes.

## Sunbit financing

Sunbit is another financing option at Sunshine Dental. As with CareCredit, you can apply at the front desk or online and get a decision in minutes.

## Our in-house payment plan

For treatment over $1,000, Sunshine Dental offers an in-house payment plan:

- **One third down** when you start.
- **The rest in 3 interest-free monthly payments.**
- **A card on file** is used for the monthly payments.

There's no interest on our in-house payment plan. Our office manager, Priya Nair, sets up payment plans and can walk you through how yours would work.

## Treatments where financing can help

Financing and payment plans are most useful for larger treatment. Some examples of our self-pay price ranges before insurance:

- Porcelain/ceramic crown (D2740): $1,150–$1,450
- Root canal, premolar (D3320): $950–$1,200
- Abutment and implant crown (D6057 + D6065): $2,100–$2,600 together
- Clear aligners, comprehensive (D8090): $3,800–$5,500

If Dr. Chen recommends treatment, you'll get a printed and emailed treatment plan with estimates, valid for 90 days. That gives you time to look at your payment options.
