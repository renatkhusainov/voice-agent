# Appointment Reminders

Sunshine Dental sends reminders before every appointment so you don't have to keep track on your own. Here's when they arrive, how to reply, and how to keep your contact details current.

## When appointment reminders are sent

You'll get appointment reminders from Sunshine Dental by **text and email**:

- **2 days before** your appointment
- **2 hours before** your appointment

The 2-day reminder gives you time to check your schedule and make changes. The 2-hour reminder is a quick heads-up on the day of your visit.

## How to reply to a reminder text

You can respond to your reminder text with a single letter:

- **Reply C** to confirm your appointment.
- **Reply R** to ask for a reschedule call.

Replying R lets our team know you'd like to change your appointment, and we'll call you to find a new time.

## Reminders and our cancellation policy

Your 2-day reminder is a good moment to check whether your appointment still works. Sunshine Dental asks for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. For Monday appointments, please let us know by Friday at 5:00 PM.

If you need to change an appointment and it's close to the time, call us at (555) 000-9999. The phone is answered 24/7.

## Keeping your contact details up to date

Reminders are only helpful if they reach you. If your mobile number or email address changes, let us know so your appointment reminders, and messages like new patient forms, go to the right place. You can:

- Call us at (555) 000-9999
- Email frontdesk@sunshinedental.example
- Tell the front desk at your next visit

## New patient forms come by text and email too

If you're a new patient, you'll also receive your new patient forms by text and email 48 hours before your first visit, with a link to the patient portal. Completing the forms online means you only need to arrive 15 minutes early instead of 30.

## Family block booking

Booking for more than one person? Sunshine Dental offers family block booking: up to 3 family members can be scheduled back to back. It's an easy way to take care of the whole family's checkups in one trip. Ask for a family block when you call (555) 000-9999.
