# Clear Aligners

Clear aligners straighten teeth with a series of nearly invisible, removable trays instead of metal braces. Dr. Mei Chen offers clear aligner treatment at Sunshine Dental for mild to moderate crowding and spacing.

## What clear aligners are

Clear aligners are thin, transparent plastic trays custom made to fit your teeth. You wear a series of aligners, and each one is shaped slightly differently, so your teeth move step by step toward their new positions. Because clear aligners are removable and hard to see, many adults prefer them to traditional braces.

## Who clear aligners are for

At Sunshine Dental, clear aligners are used for mild to moderate crowding or spacing. That includes teeth that overlap a little, or small gaps between teeth.

Whether clear aligners are a good fit for your teeth is something Dr. Chen decides after looking at your bite and your x-rays. The best first step is the free aligner consultation.

## How long clear aligner treatment takes

Typical clear aligner treatment takes 6–18 months. Dr. Chen will give you an idea of your own timeline at your consultation.
