# Preventive Care: Dental Exams and Cleanings

Regular exams and cleanings are the foundation of care at Sunshine Dental. This page explains what each visit includes, who you will see, and what exams and cleanings cost.

## Dental exams with Dr. Chen

A dental exam is when Dr. Mei Chen, DDS, checks your teeth, gums, and bite, looks at any x-rays, and talks with you about what she sees. There are two kinds of exams:

- Comprehensive exam, new patient (D0150): $95–$125
- Periodic exam, for returning patients (D0120): $55–$75

Prices on this page are self-pay ranges before insurance. Your actual share depends on your plan.

The comprehensive exam is the more thorough first look you get when you join the practice. After that, a periodic exam is the routine checkup that goes with your regular cleanings.

## Professional cleanings with Sam Rivera, RDH

Cleanings at Sunshine Dental are done by Sam Rivera, RDH, our dental hygienist.

- Adult cleaning (D1110): $105–$135
- Child cleaning (D1120): $75–$95

We see children from age 3. Children under 3 are referred to Little Teeth Pediatric Dentistry.

## What happens at a cleaning visit

At a regular cleaning, Sam Rivera removes the plaque and hardened buildup (tartar) that brushing and flossing at home can miss, then polishes your teeth. Sam also looks at your gums along the way. Dr. Chen usually stops in for your exam during the same visit.

A regular cleaning is meant for healthy gums. If your gums show signs of gum disease, Dr. Chen may recommend a deep cleaning instead, which is a different treatment with its own codes and fees.

If you feel nervous, ask about our comfort menu: headphones, blankets, and breaks whenever you raise a hand.

## How often to come in for exams and cleanings

Many patients come in for an exam and cleaning twice a year. Most PPO insurance plans cover two cleanings and exams a year at 100%, but we can't promise coverage for any specific plan. Dr. Chen and Sam Rivera will let you know what schedule fits your mouth.

You'll get text and email reminders 2 days before and 2 hours before each appointment. Reply C to confirm, or R to ask for a call to reschedule.

## Your first visit as a new patient

New patients book a "new patient visit," which includes a comprehensive exam, full x-rays, and a cleaning if your gums are healthy. Plan on 60–90 minutes.

- Arrive 15 minutes early.
- Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of your medications.
- Forms arrive by text and email 48 hours before your visit. If you can't finish them online, arrive 30 minutes early instead.
- Patients under 18 need a parent or legal guardian at the first visit.

## Exams and cleanings without insurance

If you don't have dental insurance, the Sunshine Smile Plan covers routine preventive care for a yearly fee: $349/year for adults and $279/year for children under 14. It includes 2 exams, 2 regular cleanings, all needed x-rays, 1 emergency exam per year, and 20% off other treatment. The Smile Plan isn't insurance and can't be combined with insurance, and it starts the day you sign up.
