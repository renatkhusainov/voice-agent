# Insurance Verification and Cost Estimates

Before your visit, Sunshine Dental checks your dental benefits so you have a clear idea of what you'll owe. This page explains how insurance verification works, what an estimate means, and what we need from you.

## When we verify your insurance

Sunshine Dental verifies your dental benefits **2 business days before your visit**. After checking with your plan, we give you an estimate of your share of the cost.

Because verification happens 2 business days ahead, it helps to give us your insurance details when you book, or as soon as possible after.

## What we need from you

To verify your benefits, Sunshine Dental needs:

- **Your insurance card.** Please bring it to your visit, too. New patients should also bring a photo ID and a list of medications.
- **Subscriber information.** The subscriber is the person who holds the plan. If your coverage comes through someone else, such as a spouse or parent, let us know who the subscriber is.
- **Any secondary insurance.** If you have a second dental plan, share that card as well. We bill secondary plans too.

If anything changes, such as a new job or new plan, tell us before your next visit so we verify the right coverage.

## Why an estimate isn't a guarantee

Your Sunshine Dental estimate is our best calculation of what your plan will pay and what your share will be. **The estimate isn't a guarantee: the plan decides what it pays.** Plans apply their own rules when the claim is processed, so the final amount can differ from the estimate.

For example, most PPO plans cover two cleanings and exams a year at 100%, but we can't promise coverage for any specific plan.

## Paying your estimated share

Your estimated share is due at the time of service. If the plan later pays differently than expected, any balance shows up on a statement we send by email, due within 30 days. If you end up with a credit balance, we refund it within 30 days on request.

## Treatment plans and estimates

If Dr. Chen recommends treatment, you'll receive a treatment plan with estimates. Sunshine Dental prints and emails your treatment plan, and **treatment plans are valid for 90 days**. If more than 90 days pass, ask us to update your plan and estimate.
