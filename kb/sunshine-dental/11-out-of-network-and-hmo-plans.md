# Out-of-Network PPO and HMO Dental Plans

Not every dental plan works the same way. This page explains how Sunshine Dental handles out-of-network PPO plans, which HMO-type plans we can't accept, and what to do if your plan isn't on our list.

## Out-of-network PPO plans

If you have a dental PPO plan that isn't on our in-network list, you can still be seen at Sunshine Dental. For out-of-network PPO plans:

- **We still file the claim for you.** You don't have to send paperwork to your insurance company yourself.
- **You pay the difference** between our fee and what the plan pays.

We verify your benefits 2 business days before your visit and give you an estimate, but the estimate isn't a guarantee: your plan decides what it pays.

## Our in-network PPO plans

For comparison, Sunshine Dental is in-network with Delta Dental PPO, Delta Dental Premier, Cigna Dental PPO, MetLife PDP Plus, Aetna Dental PPO, Guardian DentalGuard Preferred, and United Concordia.

## DHMO and HMO plans are not accepted

Sunshine Dental does not accept DHMO/HMO dental plans. Examples include:

- Cigna Dental Care DHMO
- Aetna DMO

An HMO-type dental plan usually requires you to see a dentist assigned by the plan. Some insurers offer both PPO and HMO plans, so look at the exact plan name on your card. For example, we accept Cigna Dental PPO but not Cigna Dental Care DHMO.

## Other plans we don't accept

Sunshine Dental also does not accept Florida Medicaid dental plans (DentaQuest, MCNA, Liberty) or CHIP / Florida Healthy Kids dental.

## What to do if your plan isn't listed

If you don't see your dental plan on our list:

1. **Check your card** for the plan type (PPO, DHMO, HMO, or DMO).
2. **Call us** at (555) 000-9999. Our office manager, Priya Nair, handles insurance and can help you figure out how your plan works with our office.
3. **If it's an out-of-network PPO,** we'll file the claim for you and you pay the difference.

## Options if your plan isn't accepted

If your plan isn't one we can accept, you can still be a patient at Sunshine Dental:

- **Pay our self-pay prices.** For example, adult cleaning (D1110) is $105–$135 and a periodic exam (D0120) is $55–$75.
- **Join the Sunshine Smile Plan,** our membership plan for patients without insurance: $349/year for adults and $279/year for children under 14. It includes 2 exams, 2 regular cleanings, all needed x-rays, 1 emergency exam per year, and 20% off other treatment. It's not insurance and can't be combined with insurance.
- **Use financing,** such as CareCredit or Sunbit, for larger treatment.
