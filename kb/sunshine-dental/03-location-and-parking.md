# Location, Parking, and Directions

Sunshine Dental is on W Kennedy Blvd in Tampa, with free garage parking right behind the building. This page explains where to park, how to get here by bus, and how to find our suite.

## Our address

Sunshine Dental
2400 W Kennedy Blvd, Suite 210
Tampa, FL 33609

Our office is in Suite 210 on the 2nd floor of the building. If you need help finding us, call (555) 000-9999.

## Free parking in the garage

The easiest place to park for Sunshine Dental is the free parking garage behind our building. The garage entrance is on S Albany Ave.

Here's how garage parking works:

- Take a ticket when you drive in.
- Bring your ticket to our front desk.
- We validate parking for up to 3 hours.

Three hours covers most visits, including a new patient visit, which usually takes 60–90 minutes.

## Accessible parking

There are two accessible parking spaces on level 1 of the garage behind our building, next to the elevator. The whole Sunshine Dental office is wheelchair accessible.

## Street parking

Metered street parking is also available on Kennedy Blvd. If you'd rather use our free validated parking, the garage behind the building (entrance on S Albany Ave) is the better choice.

## Getting here by bus

A HART bus stop is at Kennedy Blvd and Albany Ave, about 50 feet from our building's entrance. It's a short, easy walk from the bus stop to the door.

## Finding Suite 210

Once you're in the building, take the elevator from the lobby to the 2nd floor. Sunshine Dental is in Suite 210. Check in at the front desk when you arrive, and remember to bring your garage ticket so we can validate it.

If you're coming for your first visit, plan to arrive 15 minutes early (or 30 minutes early if you haven't filled in your forms online). Giving yourself a few extra minutes for parking makes the start of your visit more relaxed.
