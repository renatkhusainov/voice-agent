# Gum Disease, Deep Cleaning, and Periodontal Maintenance

Gum disease is common, and treating it usually takes more than a regular cleaning. This page explains deep cleaning (scaling and root planing), periodontal maintenance, and what each costs at Sunshine Dental.

## What gum disease is

Gum disease (also called periodontal disease) is an infection of the gums and the tissue that holds your teeth in place. It starts when plaque and tartar build up along and below the gumline. Over time, the gums can pull away from the teeth and form pockets where more buildup collects.

Only Dr. Mei Chen can tell whether you have gum disease and what treatment you need. She'll check your gums at your exam and explain her findings.

## Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing)

A deep cleaning, also called scaling and root planing, cleans below the gumline. It removes tartar from the root surfaces of your teeth and smooths the roots so the gums have a cleaner surface to heal against.

Deep cleaning is priced per quadrant. Your mouth is divided into four quadrants (upper right, upper left, lower right, lower left):

- Deep cleaning, 4 or more teeth in a quadrant (D4341): $225–$285 per quadrant
- Deep cleaning, 1–3 teeth in a quadrant (D4342): $160–$200 per quadrant

These are self-pay ranges before insurance. Your total depends on how many quadrants need treatment and how many teeth in each.

## How a deep cleaning differs from a regular cleaning

A regular adult cleaning (D1110, $105–$135) is for healthy gums. It cleans the visible parts of your teeth and just at the gumline.

A deep cleaning (D4341 or D4342) goes further, below the gumline and along the roots, to treat gum disease. Because it is more involved, it is billed by quadrant rather than as one visit. Dr. Chen will let you know which kind of cleaning is right for you.

## Periodontal maintenance after a deep cleaning

After a deep cleaning, your regular cleanings change to periodontal maintenance:

- Periodontal maintenance (D4910): $140–$175, every 3–4 months after deep cleaning

Periodontal maintenance is a more thorough cleaning than a regular one, done more often, to help keep gum disease under control. Your schedule is set by Dr. Chen and our hygienist.
