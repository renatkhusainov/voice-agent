# Dental Insurance We Accept

Sunshine Dental is in-network with several major dental PPO plans. This page lists those plans and explains how secondary insurance and preventive coverage usually work.

## In-network PPO plans

Sunshine Dental is in-network with these dental PPO plans:

- Delta Dental PPO
- Delta Dental Premier
- Cigna Dental PPO
- MetLife PDP Plus
- Aetna Dental PPO
- Guardian DentalGuard Preferred
- United Concordia

Being in-network means we have an agreement with the plan. If your plan is on this list, bring your insurance card to your visit and we'll take care of the claim.

## PPO plans vs. HMO plans from the same company

Some insurance companies offer more than one type of dental plan, so check the exact plan name on your card. For example, Sunshine Dental is in-network with Cigna Dental PPO and Aetna Dental PPO, but we don't accept DHMO/HMO plans such as Cigna Dental Care DHMO or Aetna DMO.

If you're not sure which type of plan you have, call (555) 000-9999 and we can help you check.

## Secondary insurance

If you have two dental plans, for example your own and a spouse's, Sunshine Dental bills secondary plans too. Bring both insurance cards to your visit so we can bill each plan.

## Cleanings and exams coverage

Most PPO plans cover two cleanings and exams a year at 100%. That said, every plan is different, and Sunshine Dental can't promise coverage for any specific plan. Your plan decides what it pays.

Our self-pay prices before insurance for routine preventive care:

- Periodic exam (D0120): $55–$75
- Adult cleaning (D1110): $105–$135
- Child cleaning (D1120): $75–$95

## Checking your benefits before your visit

Sunshine Dental verifies your benefits 2 business days before your visit and gives you an estimate of your share. The estimate isn't a guarantee: your plan decides what it pays. Your estimated share is due at the time of service.

## If your plan isn't listed

If your dental plan isn't on our in-network list, you may still have options. For out-of-network PPO plans, we still file the claim for you, and you pay the difference between our fee and what the plan pays. Our office manager, Priya Nair, handles insurance questions; call (555) 000-9999 and we'll help you understand your choices.
