# About Sunshine Dental

Sunshine Dental is a general family dentistry practice in Tampa, Florida, caring for patients since 2014. This page introduces our practice, our team, the specialists we work with, and how to reach us.

## Who we are

Sunshine Dental is a general family dentistry practice founded in 2014. We care for adults and for children from age 3, with everything from checkups and cleanings to fillings, crowns, whitening, and clear aligners.

Our office is at 2400 W Kennedy Blvd, Suite 210, in Tampa. Children under 3 are referred to Little Teeth Pediatric Dentistry, a practice that focuses on very young children.

## Our team at Sunshine Dental

You'll get to know a small, friendly team:

- **Dr. Mei Chen, DDS** is our general dentist and the owner of Sunshine Dental. Dr. Chen does exams, fillings, crowns, front-tooth and premolar root canals, simple and surgical extractions, implant crowns, clear aligners, night guards, and whitening. She speaks English and Mandarin.
- **Sam Rivera, RDH** is our dental hygienist. Sam does cleanings, deep cleanings, periodontal maintenance, fluoride, sealants, and whitening trays.
- **Priya Nair** is our office manager. Priya handles billing, insurance, payment plans, and records requests.
- **Lucia Gómez** is our patient coordinator. Lucia speaks Spanish and is in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## Specialists we refer to

Some care is best handled by a specialist. When you need it, Sunshine Dental refers you to trusted local partners:

- **Bayshore Oral Surgery** for impacted wisdom teeth, dental implant placement, and IV sedation.
- **Tampa Endodontics** for molar (back tooth) root canals.
- **Little Teeth Pediatric Dentistry** for children under 3.

For dental implants, Bayshore Oral Surgery places the implant, and Dr. Chen then restores it with the abutment and implant crown here at Sunshine Dental.
