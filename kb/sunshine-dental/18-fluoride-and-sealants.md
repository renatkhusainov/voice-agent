# Fluoride Varnish and Dental Sealants

Fluoride varnish and sealants are two quick, simple ways to help protect teeth from cavities. At Sunshine Dental they are offered to children and adults alike.

## What fluoride varnish is

Fluoride varnish is a protective coating painted onto the surface of your teeth. Fluoride is a mineral that helps strengthen tooth enamel, the hard outer layer of the tooth. The varnish is brushed on in a few moments, usually at the end of a cleaning, with no drilling or numbing.

- Fluoride varnish (D1208): $35–$45

This page lists self-pay ranges before insurance; your plan may pay part or all of the cost.

## What dental sealants are

A dental sealant is a thin protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth. Those surfaces have natural grooves where food and bacteria can collect, and a sealant helps cover those grooves. Placing a sealant doesn't require drilling.

- Sealant, per tooth (D1351): $45–$60

## How sealant pricing works

Sealants are priced per tooth. If you or your child have sealants placed on several teeth, the fee is the per-tooth price ($45–$60) for each tooth sealed. For example, sealants on four back teeth would be four times the per-tooth fee. We'll tell you how many teeth are recommended and give you an estimate before starting.

## Fluoride and sealants for children

Fluoride and sealants are a common part of children's dental care. We see children from age 3, and a child's preventive visit can include:

- Child cleaning (D1120): $75–$95
- Fluoride varnish (D1208): $35–$45
- Sealants (D1351): $45–$60 per tooth

Parents may stay with their children in the treatment room. If you have more than one child, family block booking lets up to 3 family members be seen back to back. Children under 3 are referred to Little Teeth Pediatric Dentistry.

## Fluoride and sealants for adults

Fluoride varnish and sealants aren't only for kids. Adults can benefit too, and Dr. Mei Chen or our hygienist may suggest them after your exam, depending on what they see in your mouth. Just ask at your next visit if you'd like to know whether either is a good fit for you.
