# Children, Pregnancy, and Your Medical History

Sunshine Dental is a family practice, and your overall health matters to your dental care. This page covers dental visits for children, our pregnancy policy, what to tell us about your health, records requests, and second opinions.

## Dental care for children

We see children from age 3. Children under 3 are referred to Little Teeth Pediatric Dentistry.

- Patients under 18 must come with a parent or legal guardian at the first visit.
- Parents may stay with their children in the treatment room.
- Family block booking lets up to 3 family members be seen back to back.

Common children's services (self-pay ranges before insurance):

- Child cleaning (D1120): $75–$95
- Fluoride varnish (D1208): $35–$45
- Sealant, per tooth (D1351): $45–$60

The Sunshine Smile Plan for children under 14 is $279/year. Please note we don't accept CHIP / Florida Healthy Kids dental or Florida Medicaid dental plans.

## Dental care during pregnancy

If you are pregnant, please tell us. Routine cleanings are fine during pregnancy. Dental x-rays are taken only when needed, and with shielding. Let us know at every visit if anything changes.

## What to tell us about your health

Your medical history helps Dr. Mei Chen plan safe care. Please tell us about:

- Pregnancy
- Heart conditions
- Joint replacements
- Blood thinners
- Diabetes
- Medication changes

Update this information at every visit, even if it seems minor. New patients fill out a medical history form, sent by text and email 48 hours before the first visit.

## Antibiotics before dental treatment

Some heart conditions and joint replacements need antibiotics before dental treatment. Whether you need antibiotic premedication is decided by your physician or Dr. Chen, not by our front desk. Please tell us about any heart condition or joint replacement before your visit so it can be sorted out ahead of time.

## Allergies and our latex-free office

Allergies, including latex and local anesthetic allergies, are noted in your chart. Our office is latex-free. Please tell us about any allergy when you book and remind us at your visit.

## Requesting your dental records

You can request copies of your dental records and x-rays with a request form at the front desk or through the patient portal at portal.sunshinedental.example.

- Records sent to another dentist are free.
- Records are ready within 5 business days.

We never share records without your written authorization, except as the law allows (for example, for treatment or payment).

## Free second-opinion visits

If another office has given you a treatment plan, you can get a free second-opinion visit at Sunshine Dental. The limited exam fee is waived if you bring a treatment plan and x-rays from another office that are less than 6 months old.
